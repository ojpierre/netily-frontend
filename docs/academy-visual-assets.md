# Academy Visual Assets

Seven generated editorial photographs are used as course thumbnails and hero backgrounds. They depict African adults learning and working with real networking equipment. These are illustrative images, not portraits of verified students or customer installations.

The generated originals are preserved outside the repository. Public assets are resized to 1440 pixels wide and compressed to WebP at quality 80. Next Image handles layout and lazy loading. The deployment currently disables image optimization globally, so enabling resized delivery variants remains a separate performance improvement.

## networking-foundations

Asset: `public/academy/networking-foundations.webp`

Generation prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: Internetily Academy course photography and background banner. Landscape 1536x1024. Scene: Black African instructor and three Black African adult learners, women and men, practising Ethernet cabling on a compact router and managed switch at a well-lit networking lab workbench. Clear faces and visible hardware, everyday professional clothing. Composition: professional editorial photograph, subjects comfortably framed in the right two thirds, uncluttered but real setting to the left for optional headline overlay. Natural daylight, true-to-life skin tones, crisp visible networking hardware, restrained blue accents. African people are the main subjects. No text, no logos, no watermark, no floating holograms, no stylised gradients. Each photograph should feel like a distinct moment in practical networking education.
